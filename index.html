Core Intelligence Modules

Six Modules. One Complete Sensory Ops Platform.

NeuroStay Engine combines Sensory Room Fingerprinting, Guest Preference Vectoring, Real-Time Risk Scoring, Stay-Outcome Learning Memory, Community Benchmarking, and PMS-Agnostic Integration into one unified SaaS platform no complex retrofits, no specialist medical knowledge required.

Module 01

Sensory Room Fingerprint Engine

Uses machine learning and spatial data analysis to map and "fingerprint" the quiet, light-controlled, and crowd-isolated rooms on a property assigning an empirical sensory index to every room.

Parses historical operational data proximity to elevator noise shafts, housekeeping cart transit patterns, street-glare, and mechanical plant vibrations to create a reusable, benchmarked hospitality asset. Once an experienced receptionist configures the optimal room for an ADHD guest, NeuroStay Engine "fingerprints" that match for instant replication by junior staff.
Module 02

Sensory-to-Stay Compatibility Matching

Automatically deconstructs unstructured guest requests into a structured Guest Preference Vector (GPV) mapping them against available room fingerprints without requiring specialist medical knowledge.

The Sensory-to-Stay Compatibility Algorithm calculates a compatibility percentage between guest needs and room environmental conditions, completely removing the need for reception staff to manually interpret cognitive or sensory requirements. Coupled with Real-Time Sensory Risk Scoring, the system identifies exactly where a room assignment breaks down before check-in.
Module 03

Stay-Outcome Learning Memory (OLM)

Post-stay feedback events are transformed into structured "Stay Intelligence Assets" mapping guest satisfaction ratings back to the original room fingerprint and preference vector.

The OLM generates comprehensive reports linking specific sensory matches to key property metrics including occupancy rates, direct bookings, and ADR impact. This provides independent hotel owners with the same level of strategic "Sensory Ops" intelligence used by multinational luxury chains at a fraction of the cost.

Platform

Six Modules. One Complete
Sensory Ops Intelligence Platform.

NeuroStay Engine combines room environmental profiling, guest preference vectoring, real-time risk scoring, stay-outcome learning, federated benchmarking, and PMS-agnostic integration into one modular SaaS platform. From reactive crisis management to proactive sensory room matching all in under three clicks.

Module 01

Sensory Room Fingerprint Engine

Maps and fingerprints every hotel room's physical and environmental profile ambient decibel peaks, lighting lux exposure, corridor layouts, and high-traffic transit routes.

Uses machine learning and spatial data analysis to assign an empirical sensory index to each room. Performance-linked room attributes are derived from real-world outcomes, creating reusable, benchmarked hospitality assets that persist through staff turnover and enable instant expert-level room allocations.
Module 02

Compatibility Matching Algorithm (GPV)

Automatically deconstructs unstructured guest reservation requests into a structured Guest Preference Vector (GPV), mapped against available room fingerprints with a compatibility percentage score.

Completely removes the need for reception staff to have medical or psychological expertise. Coupled with the Real-Time Sensory Risk Scoring Module, the system identifies exactly where a room assignment breaks down whether due to elevator noise, street-light glare, or HVAC drift and provides actionable fallback re-scoring.
Module 03

Real-Time Sensory Risk Scoring

Automatically calculates a live "Sensory Disruption Risk" score by cross-referencing upcoming hotel maintenance, adjacent building work, private events, or high-occupancy housekeeping shifts.

Proactively flags high-risk room assignments to prevent acute guest sensory overwhelm before check-in. The Sensory-to-Stay Twin Simulation virtually tests alternative room configurations and backup service pathways against a guest's preference vector during peak occupancies or unforeseen disruptions.
Module 04

Stay-Outcome Learning Memory (OLM)

Post-stay feedback events are transformed into structured "Stay Intelligence Assets" using proprietary matching algorithms that map guest satisfaction ratings back to the original room fingerprint.

The OLM is a self-optimising, closed-loop framework that continuously refines matching parameters and staff workflow SOPs for subsequent reservations. Links specific sensory matches to key property metrics occupancy rates, direct bookings, refund reductions providing direct ROI evidence for accessible tourism investment.
Module 05

Community Inclusion Network (CIN)

A privacy-first federated learning architecture enabling anonymised "Sensory-Aware Stay" outcomes and room matching patterns from UK hotel properties to constantly train matching models.

Patterns learned from successful sensory matches in historical Manchester hotels can optimise room-matching logic for independent boutique properties in Edinburgh creating a collective intelligence ecosystem. Fully compliant with UK ICO GDPR standards while building an insurmountable data moat representing the £15.3 billion Purple Pound market.
Module 06

PMS-Agnostic Integration Layer

Built to sit cleanly above existing Property Management Systems Opera PMS, Mews, Guestline, Cloudbeds via standard API syncs. Under three clicks for front-desk agents.

A lightweight browser-extension overlay reads guest booking streams from standard PMS platforms and instantly returns optimal sensory matches without requiring expensive backend API changes. Reducing property onboarding from weeks to hours, this integration layer ensures seamless adoption for the UK's busiest hotel front-desk teams.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NeuroStay Engine and what it means for your hotel's sensory operational efficiency and neuroinclusive guest experience.

What is NeuroStay Engine and why does my hotel need it?

+
NeuroStay Engine is an AI-powered operational accessibility and sensory-to-stay automation platform that solves the "Neuroinclusive Experience Gap" in UK boutique and independent hotels. Unlike standard PMS systems that treat accessibility as a static text box, NeuroStay Engine establishes a "Sensory DNA Layer" that profiles every room's environmental characteristics, matches them to guest preferences automatically, and continuously learns from stay outcomes. Pilot results showed a 28% reduction in checkout disputes and 22–35% fewer peak-hour room swaps.

How is this different from traditional PMS or accessibility directory tools?

+
Traditional PMS tools (Opera PMS, Mews) record transactions but cannot act on sensory compatibility in real time. Accessibility directories (AccessAble, WelcoMe) focus on static physical audits completely detached from live room inventory. NeuroStay Engine is the only platform that bridges the "Guest Sensory Preference" to the "Measurable Stay Outcome" predicting room mismatch risks before check-in rather than reacting to acute guest sensory overwhelm after the damage is done.

Which existing hotel systems does NeuroStay Engine integrate with?

+
NeuroStay Engine is built as a PMS-agnostic SaaS platform it sits above any underlying Property Management System including Opera PMS, Mews, Guestline, and Cloudbeds. The platform connects via a lightweight browser-extension overlay and standard API sync that captures guest booking streams from existing PMS platforms instantly, without requiring expensive backend API changes or IT-heavy implementation projects. Integration is typically completed within hours, not weeks.

What is "Sensory Room Fingerprinting" and why does it matter?

+
A Sensory Room Fingerprint is the proprietary environmental profile of each hotel room encoding its ambient decibel peaks, lighting lux levels, proximity to elevator shafts, housekeeping transit patterns, street-light glare risk, and HVAC characteristics. With a 34% guest preference loss rate during UK hotel staff turnover, this institutional knowledge vanishes every time an experienced receptionist leaves. NeuroStay Engine permanently digitises this room allocation expertise ensuring junior or newly hired staff achieve the same expert-level sensory matches instantly.

Is NeuroStay Engine compliant with UK data protection and accessibility law?

+
Yes. NeuroStay Engine is architecturally designed to comply with UK GDPR and ICO guidance on AI and Data Protection. Because guest sensory preference vectors contain sensitive, special category health data, the platform implements privacy-by-design frameworks with end-to-end tokenised database structures that store preferences separately from standard guest PII. The platform also provides audit-ready compliance trails for the UK Equality Act 2010 and the European Accessibility Act (EAA, June 2025), demonstrating active non-physical "reasonable adjustments" for non-visible disabilities.

What hotel types and sectors does NeuroStay Engine serve?

+
NeuroStay Engine is purpose-built for UK independent boutique hotels, Grade II listed heritage properties (where physical structural retrofits are legally restricted), mid-market regional hotel groups, premium serviced apartments, wellness and country house hotels, and founder-led guesthouses and B&Bs. The platform is particularly powerful for multi-property portfolios needing to prevent "operational logic loss" across rotating receptionist teams and standardise check-in workflows without capital expenditure.
